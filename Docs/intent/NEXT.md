## Slice 1 — “home is a place”

A new, empty game that Emily can play in 5 minutes:

1. Spawn in a **starter homestead**: fence ring, cottage with mass and
   a door face, walkable apron on south and west, south **interact-open**
   gate (not a quiz).
2. Walk around the house. Hug the fence in the leftover nano. Be
   occluded by the wall. No wide berth. No walking through the cottage.
3. Leave the yard. Walk to **one other place** (prefer a river with a
   **bank-to-bank** deck, or a seller cart).
4. One **teaching gate** elsewhere: an NPC asks; Book + “I don’t know”;
   unlimited retry; **correct answer opens the gate this frame**.
5. Save / load that tiny world (seed + mutations).

Entropy may be a hash for this slice. MIDI, hiss, cats, night: out of
scope.

**Done when you play it**, not when tests pass. Proof: she walks around
the house, leaves without math, a person asks a question, the gate
opens.
