# Research result

**Status: Partial**

Emily’s Game is a child-facing session product whose bar is a reliable 5–15 minute loop—spawn in an intentional place, move, face a real quiz gate, fail gently, open, leave, and enter another place—with education baked into progression on a flat 2D sim and isometric paint only. Closed campaigns (scene-first, place coherence, critical-path recovery) improved the rails but human playtest still shows the spawn→quiz→leave path broken: gates not opening on correct answers, wrong homestead quiz exits, blocked house approach, and unreadable place mass. Binding posture is stay on `experiment/isometric-2.0`, freeze FOV/nano/WorldUnitSolver thrash, and expand via scene recipes, content packs, and NPC config while fixing residual feel and critical-path defects. Recovery is a small, playtest-driven plan—not a re-run of closed epics or a greenfield engine swap.

### Product bar and core loop
The session target is short and complete: spawn → move reliably → real quiz gate → fail gently → open → leave → another intentional place, in about 5–15 minutes.[S1] The whole game is one loop—explore, collect, hit an obstacle, solve it (usually a quiz), reward, meet NPCs, reach area treasure, enter a new area—with learning as how progress works, not a bolted-on quiz mode.[S2] Quiz gates are the preferred progression obstacle: deterministic (non-LLM) verification, always retriable, wrong answers never cost progress, “I don’t know” routes to the Book of Knowledge, and softlocks are forbidden.[S6]

### Architecture and expansion laws
Authoritative simulation is a flat top-down 2D grid that owns walkability and progression; isometric view is paint-only and must never decide gate open or other gameplay state.[S3][S21] Places must be scene-first intentional compositions: free structure atoms (random towers, gate-less fence pens) are bugs; every barrier opening is `quiz_gate`, `door_locked`, or an explicit open path.[S4] Long-term expansion is limited to scene recipes, content packs (quizzes/Book articles), and NPC personas/config—not new world-engine ontology, nano systems, or solvers.[S5][S22] Product work stays on `experiment/isometric-2.0` with scene-first, place-coherence, and critical-path campaigns frozen as landed; the stated next default is content + recipes + residual feel fixes, not FOV/nano/WorldUnitSolver thrash or re-running those PR plans.[S19]

### How it has failed the design
After Critical-Path Recovery, playtest still broke the intended solve→open→leave path: answering a quiz did not open the gate; the homestead exit wrongly required a quiz; south/west house approach was blocked—restated again in the 2026-07-27 greenfield handoff as residual gate/home/art failures.[S7] Place-coherence failures came from gen stamp, walk SSOT, and draw disagreeing; after partial fixes, live play still saw mid-fence quiz_gates, free roam almost everywhere, and paint that did not read as coherent places—sealing linear gaps with `quiz_gate` was itself a product bug versus functional openings only.[S8] Boot/hang blocked session start: cold load/first frames froze the browser, Continue could stall ~30s with wait-or-kill, and static hosting could stick forever on the LLM loading splash.[S9] Mid-session softlocks froze play via quiz activation setting paused before quiz UI was active, orphan paused flags, and long frame-count illness locks—violating softlock-safe, recoverable child failure.[S10] Art/mass under locked FOV still failed readable home: flat quilted tiles, corner voids, and post-9×9 homestead reading as a small house plus rubble with blocked approach.[S11] Content-density and world-language failures left decorative atom soup—noise-like dirt/sand, lone fence posts, sparse structures, emoji salt, disconnected water tanks—instead of intentional places and readable paths.[S12]

Ranked residual defects after Critical-Path, parked at tip `784c3bf` until reopened: (1) correct quiz answer does not open the gate; (2) homestead exit must not require a quiz; (3) bare fence quiz_gates without an NPC are narrative-wrong; (4) 9×9 multi-cell cottage stamp failed child-readable home feel and blocked south/west approach; (5) walkability still feels broken at spawn—fix stamps/clearance, not FOV. Next open work should be a new small design for homestead exit + house approach + quiz resolve open, not a re-run of closed epics.[S20]

### Code structure drivers of failure
Product entry is `src/`; nested `experiment/isometric-2.0` is a parallel legacy/MCP Iso2 tree (own Vite app, types, solver, renderer)—dual-path ownership by design of the tree layout.[S13] Ported Iso2 modules in `src` still treat the experiment tree as source of truth or incomplete merge (solver/bitmask comments, merge-prototype types with TODO, texture/nano-tile origin notes), leaving ownership gaps.[S14] Downstream place failures were driven by three disagreeing pipelines—gen stamp, walk SSOT (`cell.walkable`), and draw—not a single module bug; place-coherence is a post-pipeline last writer resealing openings and illegal gaps.[S15] A residual dual walkability subsystem remains: gameplay cell SSOT versus nano/`activeConditions` APIs that previously caused snags/walk-pasts; product comments forbid global quiz-gate condition unlock controlling walkability.[S16] Chunk generation is a thrash-prone multi-phase stack (WorldUnitSolver AC-3, many gate/passability phases, place-coherence last); product law forbids expanding places via that thrash.[S17] Architecture ownership docs lag reality (`ARCHITECTURE.md` still describes `gen.ts` as a 2558-line monolith while it is a re-export facade), and automated SSOT/gate tests do not substitute for human playtest of the critical path.[S18]

### External conceptual alignment (not a drop-in swap)
An open-source isometric engine documents the same sim/render split the product laws require: design in square cartesian space while isometric “emerges,” with infinite persistent procedural map cells and NPC-focused objects—useful as conceptual reinforcement, not a TypeScript product replacement.[S23]

### Repo rails and content/CI gaps
High-value CI and content-pack rails already exist, but CI is main-branch-scoped full Playwright, screenshot refresh is manual `workflow_dispatch`, and the offline education content pipeline remains only partly automated versus its four-stage fetch→rephrase→validate→ship spec—remaining work includes rephrase quality-gate and CI-refresh, with packs already on documented paths and validate/ingest scripts.[S24]

### Recovery and continued development plan (executable posture)
**Do not** re-run scene-first, place-coherence, or critical-path campaign plans; **do** treat residual human-playtest failures as a new small epic with proof by playtest plus existing screenshot bars (`proof-place-coherence-*`, `proof-critical-path-spawn`, scene-law captures).[S19][S20][S22]

1. **Critical path (highest rank):** Make correct quiz resolution open the gate end-to-end on the real play path (not only debug/scripted tests); make homestead exit a non-quiz functional opening; ensure house apron/clearance so south/west approach is walkable without FOV change.[S7][S20]
2. **Gate narrative policy:** Prefer NPC-owned quizzes over bare mid-fence `quiz_gate`s; openings remain `quiz_gate` | `door_locked` | explicit open path only.[S4][S20]
3. **Walk SSOT hygiene:** Keep runtime authority on stamped `cell.walkable`; do not re-empower nano/`activeConditions` as walk authority; fix snags via stamps/clearance and dual-path audits, not presentation thrash.[S15][S16][S21]
4. **Place readability without ontology thrash:** Improve multi-cell home feel and reduce atom-soup language through recipes/stamps and draw budget discipline under locked FOV 128×64—not WorldUnitSolver/nano expansion.[S11][S12][S17][S19]
5. **Session start and softlock safety:** Preserve yield/budgeted load behavior; ensure quiz pause only when quiz UI is live; eliminate orphan paused/illness locks so a child can always recover.[S9][S10]
6. **Ownership cleanup (supporting, not a rewrite):** Resolve dual-tree merge debt toward `src/` as sole product entry; update architecture docs to match facades/world modules; freeze experiment tree as legacy/MCP unless a deliberate merge PR lands.[S13][S14][S18]
7. **Expand content, not engine:** Add places via catalog scene recipes + `openings[]`; add learning via content packs; add NPCs via config+dialog+sprite; complete remaining content-pipeline stages and tighten branch CI/screenshot automation as capacity allows.[S5][S22][S24]
8. **Proof bar:** Prefer scene-invariants, ban-free-structure-atoms, path-skeleton, place-coherence and critical-path screenshot proofs, gen-determinism, and playability core-loop tests—plus mandatory human play of spawn→quiz→open→leave after each residual fix.[S18][S22]

Success is a child completing one satisfying session without hang, softlock, false quiz exit, or unreadable “closed” places—then expanding the same rails with recipes and packs rather than new world ontology.[S1][S2][S19]
